What you get

Four surfaces. One customer record.

I cannot tell you much about the other software out there, because I do not use it. What I can do is tell you plainly what this does — and whose name is on it. That is this page.

It runs the day, not the boardroom. Price the job, invoice the finished work, see the schedule, and give the customer one link for everything. One workspace, whole crew, no per-user maths.

Your name goes on the paperwork. Estimates, invoices, reports and the customer portal all carry your business name and your accent colour. Nothing of ours is stamped on your work.

The four surfaces

Estimates

Build a priced quote from a template, send it as a link, let the customer approve it on their phone. Approval becomes a scheduled job.

Invoices

Invoice the finished work with your totals and terms. Card, cash or check — mark it paid and the balance leaves the books.

Dashboard

Today's jobs, what is outstanding, what is waiting on the customer. One screen for the day, printable for the truck.

Customer portal

A private link per customer: estimates to approve, invoices to pay, photos, files and a message thread. No app to install.

In every workspace

  • Your data is yours. One click to export every customer, job, invoice and estimate to CSV, and the records are never held hostage.
  • Works with no signal. Install it to your phone; when the signal drops it keeps working and uploads when you are back.
  • Team access levels. Owner, admin, dispatcher and crew, so the office sees money and the crew sees the day.
  • Document templates. Pick a template and an accent colour once; every estimate, invoice and report follows it.
  • Free tools with no account. Invoice, estimate, QR code, before/after photo compare and a printable chimney inspection checklist.
