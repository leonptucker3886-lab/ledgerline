Kickserv's entry plan is $60/mo for 5 users. A solo sweep or a two-person crew pays for seats nobody sits in. Ledgerline is one flat price and your whole crew is included.

The next step up nearly doubles.

Going from Start to Run is $60 to $119/mo to pick up 5 more seats. Ledgerline does not re-price you when a helper, a sub or your kid joins the crew.

Discounts with conditions attached.

The advertised 20% is an annual prepay, and the 5% comes with online payment volume requirements. Ledgerline is a flat price on 6-month terms, with no volume you have to hit.
